# The world after the beginning

This is the map after the opening cinematic.
Erathma, the human continent, is gone.

Banner text on the painting:
ERATHMA WAS DESTROYED. ITS LANDS WERE DIVIDED. SEVEN KINGDOMS ROSE FROM ITS RUINS.

## What happened
Erathma stood at the heart of the world. Kingdoms, culture, power.
Ambition, war, and forbidden magics — the Cataclysm — broke the land and the unity of man.
Seven powers carved what they could hold.
The name Erathma survives in histories and ruins. Not on flags.

Ash does not know this as his story. The player may see ruins. Nobody explains the blood in Year 1.
Do not title him Last Prince of Erathma on Boot.

## The seven that remain
1. **Draconia** — northern realms. Capital Draktharion. Claimed former northern Erathma.
2. **Kitasa** — eastern provinces. Capital Inxyo. Claimed former eastern kingdoms.
3. **Lumora** — western provinces. Capital Velmoria. Claimed former western kingdoms.
4. **Narigoth** — western highlands. Capital Narigoth. Claimed the highlands.
5. **Ernholt** — southern / central heartlands. Capital Ernholt. Claimed the old heart.
6. **Destra** — southeastern wilds. Capital Destra.
7. **Babathia** — southeastern isles. Capital Dreamwhisk. The only land still mostly water-cut from the rest.

Peoples still match those names. They are kingdoms on a corpse, not eight separate worlds.
