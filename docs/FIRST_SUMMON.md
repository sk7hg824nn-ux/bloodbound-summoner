# First summon — locked beat

Summoning is a RITUAL, not a shout.

Ash performs the rite in the wilderness, alone, as he understands it.
Nothing answers. He believes the ritual failed. He walks away.
A low-level monster starts its strike.
Before the blow lands, Akari destroys it.

He does not fight. He does not take the hit.
She was already there. The rite was not empty in the way he thought.
He thinks he failed. She knows something happened.

Do not invent ritual components, gestures, or chalk until the story names them.
The academy circle is not this meeting.
