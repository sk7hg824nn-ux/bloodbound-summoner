# First summon — locked beat

Ash is timid.

1. Academy RITUAL CHAMBER. Official summoning in front of everyone.
   Stone circle already in the floor. Drop of his blood. The rite fails. Humiliation.
2. A week later. He tries again in the woods, alone.
   He draws a circle. He gives a drop. Nothing answers. He walks away.
3. A low-level monster starts its strike.
4. Before the blow lands, Akari destroys it.

He does not fight. He thinks both rites failed. She knows something happened.
The chamber is public. The woods are private. Same price. Two rooms.
