# First summon — locked beat

Ash is timid.

1. Academy ritual chamber. Bleachers full of students.
   Official summoning in front of everyone. Floor circle. Drop of blood.
   The rite fails. The crowd is the joke.
2. A week later. Woods. Alone. Circle. Drop. Nothing. He walks away.
3. A low-level monster starts its strike.
4. Before the blow lands, Akari destroys it.

He thinks both rites failed. She knows something happened.
