# First summon — locked beat

Ash calls in the wilderness. Nothing answers. He believes he failed. He walks away.
Then a low-level monster starts its strike.
Before the blow lands, Akari destroys it.

He does not fight. He does not take the hit. Summon 1 ends the creature.

She was already there. Contempt, not service. He thinks he failed. She knows something happened.
The academy circle is not this meeting.
