# First summon — locked beat

Ash is timid.

1. Grand hall. Bleachers. Another student succeeds first.
   Blood to the seal. A griffin answers. Successful pact.
   Then they call Ash. Same blade. Same drop. Twice. Nothing they count.
   Other summons flinch. He does not know why. The crowd laughs.
2. A week later. Woods. Alone. Circle. Drop. Nothing. He walks away.
3. A low-level monster starts its strike.
4. Before the blow lands, Akari destroys it.

He thinks both rites failed. They are afraid of his blood. He is not told that here.
The griffin is not his companion.
