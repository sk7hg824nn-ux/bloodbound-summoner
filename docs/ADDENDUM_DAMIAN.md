# Addendum — Damian, Elizabet, the monsters

Bloodbound only. Design only. Player brick locked.

## Elizabet
Damian's Queen. Spelling: Elizabet. Terminal illness. No cure. His immortality work began as a bid to save her. That origin does not excuse the rest.

## Experiments
People first, then family. Mutations. Some subjects became monstrous. That is the origin of the creatures on the outskirts.
He would not kill them. He released them. Guilt without repair. The line continued.

## Hidden fact (do not reveal early)
Offspring of those creatures still carry part-human ancestry. The world sees monsters. Nobody casual knows the human line. Ash does not know.

## Ash's childhood
Raised in the outskirts among those creatures. To him they are just what lives near home. He does not know Damian made the first ones, that the subjects were human, that the blood in the brush is family business, or that Damian is his grandfather.

## Julian
Discovered the work. Banished Damian. Burned lab and research. Damian lived. That road leads to the seven and the fall of Erathma.

## Do not ship as systems
No mutation tree, monster-ancestry stat, Damian morality meter, secret Ash-monster power, or early reveal quest. Story facts until a chapter earns them.
