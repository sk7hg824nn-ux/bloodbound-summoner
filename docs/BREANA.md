# Breana — third summon

Bloodbound only. Design only. Player brick locked.
Supersedes the old campaign name "Mimi" for this character.

## Who
Ash's third pact. Yandere bunny girl.
Obsessed with love and fantasy. Daydreams hard. Treats the summoning as fate. Ash is the only one. Possessive. Protective. Fights with Akari and Lithanya as a sport. Romance in her head can be a comedy. The dark edge is not a joke.

## The line
"He's mine" is the light surface.
If she believes someone will harm him or take him, she is willing to kill.
Comedy is real. Obsession is real. Danger is real. Akari and Lithanya are allowed to see that.
Akari: she is insane.
Lithanya: tries to put a theory on it.
Breana: argues about Ash while making the threat plain.

## Three languages (character, not kits)
- Akari — pride / bond
- Lithanya — knowledge / love
- Breana — fate / obsession
Clash is constant. Found family is allowed. Nobody is reduced to "the jealous girl."

## Unpinned
When she appears. When the pact seals. Her combat language. Whether kill-intent ever becomes a playable verb. Do not invent a bunny kit to fill a slot.
