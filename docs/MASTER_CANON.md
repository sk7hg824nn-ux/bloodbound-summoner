# Bloodbound master canon

Not Duskfable. Design only. Player brick locked. No Pact+Combat code until BUILD IT.

## World
Eight kingdoms: Erathma, Draconia, Kitasa, Lumora, Narigoth, Ernholt, Destra, Babathia.
Erathma was the crown jewel. Destroyed. Lands divided among the other seven. World forgot it.
The seven were deceived by Damian Dracmoth. They are not inherently evil.
Truth survives only as fragments. Do not invent specific relics until the story names them.

## House Dracmoth
- Julian Dracmoth — King of Erathma. Father. Banished Damian, destroyed the lab and research.
- Elissa Dracmoth — Queen. Mother. 18 and pregnant when Erathma fell. Fled. Hid the child's name.
- Protagonist — lost Prince. True surname Dracmoth. Does not know it. Does not know parents' titles. Does not know Erathma. Identity stays hidden until the final confrontation.
- Damian Dracmoth — grandfather. Immortality experiments on Erathma and on family. Survived exile. Manipulated the seven. Final boss. His monologue is the complete reveal.

## Loyalists
A hidden sect knows Erathma and the line survived. They hunt the Prince. The world calls them cultists. They have fragments, not the whole file. Ash does not know they exist. They may meet him and miss him.

## Academy
The Illustrious Academy stands on the site of Castle Erathma. He walks it every day. He does not know.

## Summons and pacts
Multiple summons exist in the world. Rank E→C→D→B→A→S→SS→SSS. S+ is not normal loot.
Ash: three SSS-potential pacts, all born E.
Pact is a two-way contract and the fight. Akari is the combatant, not a damage pet. No empty-handed win. Tails are story facts and new kinds of lie, not damage × n. Bond is willingness, not +50%.
One sealed pact on the first combat brick. No party bar until the story says so.
