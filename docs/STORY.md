# The Bloodbound Summoner — main campaign

Encoded in `StoryDB`, `Campaign`, `Act1`, and `LaterActs`.

The world thinks the Bloodbound vanished. They were feared for commanding enormous magic. The protagonist is the last descendant and does not know it. Ordinary summons will not answer because the blood refuses a master-and-pet contract. The forgotten art is the Blood Pact: a chosen bond with a living person. Power flows both ways. Deeper bond, stronger both.

## Playable now (Act I)

1. **Entrance exam** — wolf, whelp dragon, elemental knight, then you. Nothing. Official record: insufficient. The school joke.
2. **Rin** — cheers you, is secretly embarrassed.
3. **Private practice at Foxwood Gate** — last plea. One-tailed kitsune appears. She cannot sense your power. You beg. She makes you beg again. Pact seals. Power leaks both ways. Neither understands. Academy stamps her as your first summon. You are ecstatic. She is unimpressed.
4. **Beginner tournament** — expected to lose instantly. Win two. New foxfire. Lose the final because you do not understand the new language yet. Cael notices potential, not power.

## Rule that must not break

The protagonist and the player discover the truth together. Sera learns "living bonds" before you do and stays quiet. "Blood Heir" is the first time the world names you. The First King is the first time anyone says descendant.
