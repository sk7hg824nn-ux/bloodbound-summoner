# Rin — locked canon (owner, 2026-10-19)

Ash's childhood friend from the outskirts. Same age as Ash. Daughter of Torin and Maren.

## Her summon
- **Coral**, a Rank A sea serpent, leviathan type. Blue-green scales the size of shields, a long finned head crowned with coral-like frills, pale gold eyes.
- Rank A is very rare: Rin becomes the academy's first Rank A in years, overnight.
- Coral answers in the grand-hall summoning in Book Two, **right before Ash's empty circle**: seawater bursts from the seal, the leviathan coils around Rin three times, Rin lays her bleeding hand on its jaw and names it Coral.
- When Ash's blood draws nothing, Coral flinches with every other summon in the hall.
- Coral is too big for the halls and lives in **the Mere**, the deep lake below the academy cliffs; crowds gather on the shore to watch her surface when Rin calls.
- A leviathan cannot fight on sand: Rin competes in the **Tidewater Cup**, the water bracket fought on floating rings on the Mere. She wins it in Year One.

## Who she is
- Famous overnight, courted by noble students, and still sits with Ash every time.
- Cheers for Ash when he is the school joke, and is secretly embarrassed to.

## Between Book One and Book Two
- Ash's mother dies when he is 15; he enters the academy at 18. For those three years he lives alone in the cottage, door barred. Torin keeps coming to fix the doors; Maren sends warm bread every few days. Rin keeps climbing the crooked fence.
- The day they leave for the academy, Maren cries at the end of the lane and pretends it is the wind; Torin puts a hand on Ash's shoulder: "Bring her back in one piece."
