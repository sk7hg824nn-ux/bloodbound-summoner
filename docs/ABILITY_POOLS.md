World: illusion, deception, perception, bond.
Pool: false targets, later shapeshift (probably not combat), foxfire as attention not DoT, dream appearances, possession only with extreme care, disguise, tells (wrong shadow, wrong reflection, tail under the hem, foxfire leak).

## Lithanya — Dragoness

World: knowledge, ancient force, history. Element TBD by Draconia, not every dragon power at once.
Pool: body, flight, fire or water/storm if the culture says so. Her real first gift may be reading a fragment nobody else can. That is not a fireball.
No locked combat kit.

## Breana — Bunny

Pool: coincidence, timing, protective snap, moon/jade-rabbit imagery, trickster unpredictability. Not bunny-plus-knife as a class. Not reality-break probability unless a chapter earns it.
No locked combat kit.
