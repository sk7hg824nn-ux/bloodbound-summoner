# Lithanya — second pact

Bloodbound only. Design only. Player brick locked.
Supersedes the old campaign name "Sera" for this character.

## Who
Dragoness of Draconia. Royal. Seventh in line. She knows the throne is not coming and does not scheme for it. She spends that life on books.

## Mind
Extreme bookworm. Ancient languages, lost cities, broken inscriptions, magical theory, anything that outlived its speakers. If the world shrugged at a fragment, she wants the line.
Erathma is scattered trash to most people. She is the one who might notice the word Dracmoth. She does not know Ash is the Prince when she starts looking. Do not make her a walking lore dump.

## Heart
She falls in love with Ash the outskirts student. Not the blood, not the name, not SSS potential. She does not have the Prince file when the feeling starts. The end-game reveal hits because of that.
Brilliant at dead languages. Clumsy at her own chest. Akari will see it. Ash may not.

## Combat
Not Kitsune-with-scales. Language still unpinned. Directions only: body, flight, fire, space, Draconian craft, knowledge. No locked kit until the story names a verb.

## With Akari
Proud fox vs scholar who is obvious about Ash. Rivalry and comedy are allowed. Neither girl is "the jealous one" as a job title.
