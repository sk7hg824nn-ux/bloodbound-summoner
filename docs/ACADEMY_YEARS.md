# Four years at the Illustrious Academy

Bloodbound only. Built from locked canon. Ash does not learn he is Prince of Erathma here. Damian does not give the speech here. The school sits on Castle Erathma and nobody says that sentence out loud.

Retired on this sheet: Sera, Mimi, First King as the end-game name.

Still unpinned (do not fill): Akari's rival's name, the first nine-tail's name, specific relics, Lithanya's age, mid-fight multi-pact rules, Breana's homeland.

---

## The school

The Illustrious Academy trains students from the seven living kingdoms to battle through registered summons. Rank talk is cheap at E–B and gets quiet at A. S is a scandal. SSS is a ghost story.

Ash enrolls as an outskirts first-year with no surname the ledger respects. Mother's rule still holds in his mouth: if anyone asks, he is her child.

Rin is the classmate who cheers and is embarrassed. Cael is the one who studies people instead of gossip. The Dean enforces handbook law: a registered summon may share the dorm. That rule will matter when three living people, not pets, sleep on his floor.

## Year One — Insufficient

**Locked beats**

- Entrance circle. Other students call a wolf, a whelp dragon, an elemental knight. Ash's circle stays empty. Stamp: insufficient. He becomes the school joke.
- Rin cheers anyway.
- Foxwood Gate. Last plea. Akari, one tail, Princess of Kitasa, shrine-maiden pride. She cannot sense the blood. He begs twice. Pact seals. Ledger writes first summon, rank E. She is not impressed.
- Beginner ring / tournament. Expected to vanish. They win two because the enemy hits the lie. They lose the final because he still points like a fist and she will not carry a fool through a title match.
- Cael notices *potential*, not power. He does not say Blood Heir.

**Year-one life**

Classes assume a student commands a creature. Ash has a princess who talks back in the dorm. Akari looks down on the outskirts and on the joke. She trains by Kitasa doctrine: drill, meditation, one tail. The pact leaks both ways in small ugly ways (a headache when she is furious, a flinch when he is scared). Neither has a word for it.

No second tail this year. A foxfire crumb may flicker once in the final and die. That is not a new verb and not a new tail. They do not understand it. The shrine book does not list it.

Lithanya and Breana are not here yet as pacts. A Draconian transfer may exist in the library as a rumor of a seventh daughter who reads dead languages. Ash does not have that conversation yet.

**What Ash knows at year's end**

The circle was empty because the air will not take a pet. A proud fox answered a beggar. He can stand, call one lie, and cut. He is not ordinary. He still cannot name why. He is not a king.

## Year Two — The book and the second tail

**From canon**

Lithanya of Draconia, seventh in line, bookworm, not hunting a throne. She falls for Ash the student, not a title she does not have. She may notice the word Dracmoth in a scrap and not know the boy beside her owns it. Akari sees the crush immediately. Ash may not.

Second tail manifests after the bond with Ash has actually changed something. Akari's unnamed rival says she cheated. Doctrine has no slot for it. Elders may refuse the possibility.

**Year-two shape**

- Library, not a throne room. Lithanya translates junk. One fragment does not say "you are the prince." It says a dead house-name, a burned jewel-kingdom, a date that does not match the seven kingdoms' textbooks. She files it under "wrong catalog." She does not give a lecture.
- Pact two seals when the story has made her choose the person, not the puzzle. Rank E. Dragon language still mostly body and presence; no element kit until Draconia names it.
- Akari's second tail happens on a training green with the rival watching. Public crack in Kitsune history. Akari is angry it happened *near him*, not grateful.
- Dorm handbook gets stress-tested: two registered summons, two personalities, one embarrassed first-year.
- Tournament year two: better lies, still one combatant at a time unless a later line says otherwise. They are not SSS. They are E climbing toward C because the work is real.

**What Ash knows at year's end**

There was a kingdom the textbooks skip. A shy dragon loves books and, inconveniently, him. His fox grew a tail the shrine calls impossible. He still answers to Ash from the outskirts.

## Year Three — Fate walks in

**From canon**

Breana, third pact. Yandere bunny. Fate, one-and-only, comedy and kill-line both real. Argues with Akari and Lithanya. Akari: insane. Lithanya: footnotes it. Found family allowed. Soft yandere not allowed.

Loyalists exist in the world. They may pass the academy and miss the Prince. The wider school calls such people cultists.

**Year-three shape**

- Breana arrives as if the meeting were already written. She treats the pact as destiny. Rank E. Combat language unpinned; her first public fact is not a kit, it is that she will stand between Ash and a threat and mean it.
- Three-way clash becomes daily weather. Pride / knowledge / fate. They still function when a match requires a choice of who walks onto the sand. No party-swap bar until canon says so.
- A loyalist preacher on the town road talks about a lost jewel and a surviving son. Classmates laugh. Lithanya's notes twitch. Ash hears a ghost story about someone else.
- Upper-bracket tournament. Ranks may touch D/C for the three if the year has earned sweat, not if a table said so. Other students still do not own S as a hobby.
- Late year: someone uses the words **Blood Heir** at him without the rest of the file. Accusation, raid, or lockdown seed — not Damian's monologue. "You should never have awakened" can be said by a hunter or a frightened teacher. Ash still does not own Dracmoth.

**What Ash knows at year's end**

Three living bonds. People are hunting a story that sounds like his mother's warning. The school is not as safe as the brochure. He still does not know the castle under the lecture hall is his.

## Year Four — The site remembers

**From canon**

The academy is Castle Erathma's footprint. Fragments only. Seven kingdoms were deceived, not born wicked. Loyalists have pieces. Complete truth waits for Damian at the end, off this calendar if the war leaves the grounds.

**Year-four shape**

- Senior work. The three are not maxed. SSS is still potential, not a badge on the uniform.
- Lithanya can connect two fragments (a royal mark, a foundation stone under the east wing) and go pale. She does not complete the sentence "you are the prince" unless you later pin that she almost does and stops. Safer lock: she knows the *site* is older than the school. She does not dump the family tree on him.
- Akari's doctrine war follows her from Kitasa. Bond versus meditation becomes a political problem, not a damage node.
- Breana decides the hunt is a threat to *hers*. Comedy dies for a scene. The other two see the kill-line without a joke attached.
- Rin goes to the Dean when dorm law and rumor collide. Cael has research, not a heel turn.
- Year ends in crisis, not graduation comfort: lockdown, evacuation, or a journey off campus toward the people who remember Erathma wrong. The final boss and the full name wait after the gates, or on the last night, but the **complete** file stays Damian's.

**What Ash must still not know when year four closes, unless you later move the final fight onto campus**

- Surname Dracmoth
- Julian was king, Elissa queen
- Damian is grandfather
- The monsters of the outskirts were his experiments
- The seven were puppets

He may suspect the school is standing on a grave. That is not the monologue.

## How the years teach combat without breaking the pact rule

Year 1: one pact, one tail, one lie.
Year 2: second tail as story crack; Lithanya's language begins if the pact exists; still no fist.
Year 3: third pact exists; who walks into the ring is a relationship fight as much as a match.
Year 4: coordination the plot has earned, not a gacha party.

Tails and ranks move when the year has a reason. Never `level → +1 tail → +damage`.

## Classmates from the seven
