# Bloodbound — JRPG direction

Feel: a full traditional JRPG adventure. Presentation: illustrated 2.5D.
2.5D is not "2D plus sparkles." The world has depth. Layers, parallax, camera push-ins, light, rooms that read as places.
It is also not a MeshInstance open world.

## Exploration (later bricks)
Towns, villages, academy, forests, mountains, ruins, dungeons, caves, castles, secrets, world-map travel.
Year 1 on the phone is still: cinematic prologue → hall exam → woods pact. Do not stand up the map until that loop is true.

## Story
Long cinematics. Character talk. Revelations. Party talk. Romance. Betrayal. Loss. Humor. Revenge. Arcs.
Ash knows nothing at the start. Lithanya's research is what begins to name the blood.

## Combat (still one system with Pact)
Stats, gear, abilities, elements when the story names them, status, crits, bosses, companion verbs, pact verbs, combinations.
Empty hands still do not fight. Akari is the first combatant. Lithanya and Breana stay closed until their chapters.
Tails are not a damage stat.

## Progression
Level, XP, attributes, skills, gear, abilities.
Bond. Akari tails. Lithanya dragon form. Breana obsession kit. Ash's Bloodbound line.
Each of those is earned in story, not a menu at Boot.

## Do not smash this into Year 1
Player brick stays locked.
No world map this slice.
No elemental chart until a chapter writes it.
