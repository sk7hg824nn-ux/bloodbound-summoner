# Presentation standard — locked

The field should read like:
- Star Ocean Second Story R: a real place you walk through. Path, fence, town, hills. Party on the road.
- Another Eden: a painted plate with depth. Small figures on a designed floor.
- HD-2D towns (Octopath-class): foreground sits in front of the actors. Light has a direction.

Talk should read like:
- Tales portrait boards. Faces carry the line. Not a nameless bar on black.

## What we take
Environment is a painting (or layered paintings), not a ColorRect.
Actors stand on that painting.
Camera can sit at a slight 3/4.
Foreground objects and blur sell depth.
Dialogue uses portraits and expressions from the locked sheets.

## What we do not take
Those games' plots, jobs, towns, or characters.
Octopath pixel as Ash's body. Field may be small; the approved sheets stay illustrated anime.
MeshInstance heroes.
A black subtitle over a void as the finished cutscene language.

## Honest gap
Year 1 on the phone is still placeholder plates plus a silhouette Ash.
This file is the bar. HallArt / OriginArt are not the bar.
Required assets: painted hall plate, painted woods, painted prologue stills, field sprites, talk portraits.
Until those PNGs are in res://art and imported, the standard is unmet.
